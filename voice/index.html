American English text-to-speech tool

Turn English text into speech for pronunciation practice, presentations and listening. Choose an American English voice, adjust the speaking rate, and download WAV audio.

Speech synthesis runs in your browser using Kokoro. Text is not sent to a speech API. The first neural-voice session downloads the model from this website; saved recordings stay in the current browser.

Created by composer Quanzhong Deng.
