02 / BEHIND THE MUSIC

About the artist.

个人简介

Quanzhong Deng is pursuing a D.M.A. in Composition at the University of Missouri–Kansas City, where he studies under distinguished composers Chen Yi, Zhou Long, and Paul Rudy. At UMKC, he also serves as the President of the Composers Guild, where he oversees all aspects of concert production, including managing calls for scores, programming, marketing, and coordinating residency artists. He earned a B.A. from the Sichuan Conservatory of Music. He has received multiple accolades, including Chinese National Scholarships and prizes in both international and Chinese composition competitions. His work has been featured at prestigious events such as the St. Petersburg International New Music Festival, Asian Classical Music Initiative, SPLICE Institute, and Oregon Bach Festival Composers Symposium.

Deng's collaborative experience spans film scoring, concert adaptations, and performances with renowned ensembles, including the MolOt Ensemble, Chongqing Philharmonic Orchestra, China-ASEAN Contemporary Ensemble, Del Sol Quartet, Chatter PDX, Unheard-of//Ensemble, Yuan-Yin Duo, and Crossroad Saxophone Quartet, among others. His compositions reflect a unique blend of traditional and contemporary musical elements.

邓荃中，现为密苏里大学堪萨斯城校区作曲专业博士研究生，师从陈怡教授，周龙教授与保罗·鲁迪教授。在校期间担任 UMKC 作曲家协会（Composers Guild）主席，负责统筹音乐会制作的全面工作，包括管理作品征集、曲目策划、宣传推广以及协调驻校艺术家等。本科毕业于四川音乐学院作曲系，先后师从肖岗教授与刘鹏副教授，国家奖学金获得者。多次在国内外作曲比赛中获奖，包括阿马德乌斯国际作曲比赛二等奖（一等奖空缺）、第十届塞尔维亚斯梅代雷沃国际作曲比赛二等奖、第十二届中国东盟音乐周暨“花山杯”作曲比赛三等奖、“岭南风格”中国钢琴新作品作曲比赛优秀奖等。

其作品曾在美国、中国、俄罗斯等地演出，合作团体包括东盟当代乐团、重庆爱乐乐团、MolOt Ensemble、Illusion Duo、Del Sol Quartet等，并与吉他演奏家匡俊宏、女高音歌唱家黄英合作。曾参与 Andre Mark, Dmitri Kourliandski, Januibe Tejera, Michael Pisaro 等国际作曲家的大师班，并为 China Daily Legal 出品的普法动画剧集《小治小宝寻法记》创作全套配乐，该动画获法制网、人民网、腾讯网、澎湃要闻等媒体首页推荐报道。
